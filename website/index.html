01 — The route

Your context goes straight to your provider.

Selected context goes to the provider you configured. There is no relay and no analytics SDK. Live screen and audio stay in memory; saved transcripts stay on this device.

02 — What it does

Screen help, meeting capture, Task Context.

Three ways to give the panel context.

Screen help

Ask about what is already on screen

Ask about an error, a diff or a dense document without retyping it. The screen is captured locally and sent only when you ask.

Meeting capture

Meeting transcripts, with You and Them separated

Separate You and Them channels, searchable history on your device, and notes only when you ask for them.

05 — Before you install

Before you install.

The things the installer and the app cannot decide for you.

macOS builds are unsigned

Open the app once, then approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security on macOS 15 or later; on macOS 13–14, right-click and choose Open. xattr -cr is an advanced fallback. The panel has no Dock icon, so reopen it from Applications. An update may ask for Screen Recording permission again.

Windows shows an unknown publisher warning

The Windows installer is unsigned. SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC"; choose More info and then Run anyway.

Updates check GitHub every six hours

Volyx Lens asks api.github.com for the latest release. The request includes your IP address and user agent. Unsigned Windows and macOS builds only notify; Linux AppImages update in place, and nothing installs without your action. Turn update checks off in Settings → Updates.

Keys are stored on your machine, not on a Volyx server

Credentials use macOS Keychain, Windows DPAPI, or Linux libsecret/KWallet. If secure storage is unavailable, keys stay in memory for the session and are never written to disk.

What works offline

Ollama gives offline responses. Local Whisper gives offline batch transcription on Windows and Linux after you enable it and download a model; macOS needs a configured whisper-server binary.

Recording law is still your responsibility

Capture permissions are explicit, but consent and recording laws still apply. Do not capture people without their permission. Capture exclusion is best-effort and never guaranteed.
